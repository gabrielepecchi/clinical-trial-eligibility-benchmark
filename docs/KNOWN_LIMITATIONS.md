# Known Limitations

This document describes the known limitations of the
**clinical-trial-eligibility-benchmark** project.

---

## Project scope

This is a research and portfolio benchmark, not a clinical decision-support
system. It is intended to demonstrate reproducible evaluation workflows and
benchmark engineering practices. No part of this project has been clinically
validated or reviewed by licensed clinicians.

---

## Patient data

- All patient cases are **synthetic**. No real patient records or protected
  health information (PHI) are intended to be used or included anywhere in
  this project.
- Synthetic patient profiles are designed to cover common Parkinson disease
  trial eligibility dimensions, but their distribution does not necessarily
  reflect real-world patient populations.

---

## Labels

- Labels are **LLM-reviewed draft benchmark labels**, not clinician-adjudicated
  gold standard labels.
- No physician review, IRB approval, or formal annotation protocol has been
  applied.
- Label quality depends on the LLM review process at the time the labels were
  generated and may contain errors or inconsistencies.

---

## Trial data

- Trial criteria are derived from public clinical trial records
  (ClinicalTrials.gov-derived content where applicable).
- Criteria text may be incomplete, outdated, or not representative of current
  trial versions.

---

## Matcher limitations

The matcher is rule-based and applies heuristic logic. It may miss or
mishandle complex clinical reasoning. Known blind spots surfaced by project
checks include:

- **MAO-B inhibitor exclusions** may not always be detected, particularly when
  drug names are listed by brand name or partial synonym.
- **Prior DBS / prior procedure exclusions** may not always be detected when
  procedure history is described indirectly or using variant terminology.
- **Cognitive / MoCA thresholds** may not always be detected when score fields
  are absent or formatted unexpectedly.
- **Implanted device / pacemaker exclusions** may not always be detected when
  device history is described with non-standard phrasing.
- **Medication stability and missing medication history** may remain
  unresolved, leading to unclear predictions where a more informed system
  might reach a definite conclusion.

The matcher does not perform deep semantic reasoning, temporal inference over
real clinical dates, or multi-step logical composition beyond its implemented
rules.

---

## Reports and analysis scripts

- The reports generated by this project (coverage analysis, label disagreement,
  error analysis, HTML report, etc.) are **audit and analysis tools**, not
  evidence of clinical validity.
- All generated reports reflect the current snapshot of processed data. If
  source files change, reports must be regenerated to remain accurate.

---

## Confidence scores

- Confidence scores in prediction outputs are heuristic or model-output fields.
- They should **not** be interpreted as calibrated probabilities unless
  specifically validated against a held-out dataset with calibration analysis.

---

## Appropriate use

This project is appropriate for:

- Portfolio demonstration and benchmark engineering showcase.
- Reproducible evaluation workflow development.
- Error analysis and failure mode documentation.
- Experimenting with rule-based eligibility matching approaches.
- Academic or personal research on clinical NLP benchmarking methodology.

---

## Not appropriate for

This project is **not** appropriate for:

- Patient care or any clinical decision-making.
- Determining eligibility for real clinical trials.
- Regulatory submissions or clinical claims of any kind.
- Replacing expert medical or legal review.
- Any production deployment involving real patients or trial operators.
